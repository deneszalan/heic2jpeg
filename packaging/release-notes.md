## Download

1. Download **HEIC-to-JPEG-Converter.exe** below (under *Assets*).
2. Double-click it. There is nothing to install.

If Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info → Run anyway**. This appears because the program is new and not code-signed.

## How to use

Drag your HEIC photos or folders onto the window, press **Convert**, then click **Open folder** to see the JPEGs. Your original photos are never changed.
